The dashboard answers three questions: how compliant are we, what is urgent, and what do I need to do?

## Readiness

One card per framework you've turned on, showing the readiness percentage: met requirements out of applicable ones. Click a framework to see its requirements and which controls hold each one back. How the number is calculated is explained in [Key concepts](/docs/concepts#how-readiness-is-calculated).

## Deadlines

Legal and operational deadlines, soonest first, with overdue items highlighted:

- **CERT-In reports**: 6 hours from when a reportable incident was detected;
- **Data Protection Board reports**: the detailed report 72 hours after a personal data breach was detected;
- **Notifying affected people** about a personal data breach;
- **Rights requests** (DPR numbers) approaching their due date;
- **Tasks** with due dates.

## Controls that need attention

Up to eight controls that are **Failing check** or **Needs evidence**, failing ones first. Open one to see why: the failing check's details or the missing evidence.

## Controls summary

A bar showing how many controls are passing, failing, need evidence, are in progress, not started or not applicable. Use the filters on the **Controls** page to list the controls in any of these states.

## Your work

- **My tasks**: your open tasks, soonest due first.
- **Policies to read and acknowledge**: approved policies you haven't acknowledged in their current version.
- **Vendor reviews due**: vendors whose next review date falls in the next 30 days.

## Recent activity

The latest entries from the audit log: who changed what and when.
