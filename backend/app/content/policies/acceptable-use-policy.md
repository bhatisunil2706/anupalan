# Acceptable Use Policy

**Company:** {{company}}
**Owner:** {{owner}}
**Applies to:** All employees and contractors using {{company}} devices, accounts, networks or data.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how you may and may not use {{company}} systems and information.

## 2. General rules

1. Use company systems mainly for business purposes. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work or security.
2. Use only company-approved tools to store or share company and customer data.
3. Never share your passwords or MFA codes. Use the company password manager.
4. Lock your screen when you step away. Devices lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
5. Keep devices updated and do not disable encryption, endpoint protection or MDM.
6. Do not install unlicensed or unapproved software.

## 3. Personal data and confidential information

1. Access personal data only when your role needs it.
2. Do not copy customer or employee personal data to personal devices, personal email or personal cloud storage.
3. Do not use real personal data for testing unless it is masked.
4. Do not paste confidential or personal data into public AI tools or websites unless the tool is approved.

## 4. Remote working

1. Work only on company-managed devices.
2. Avoid untrusted public Wi-Fi; use a mobile hotspot or company VPN where required.
3. Keep screens out of view of others and do not print confidential information at home.

## 5. Prohibited use

You must not use company systems to break the law, harass others, access content unrelated to work that creates security risk, attempt to bypass security controls, or access data you are not authorised to see.

## 6. Monitoring

{{company}} monitors its systems for security purposes, in line with applicable law and our employee privacy notice.

## 7. Acknowledgment

All staff acknowledge this policy when they join and once a year.
