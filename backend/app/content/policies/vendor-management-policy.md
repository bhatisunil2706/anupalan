# Vendor Management Policy

**Company:** {{company}}
**Owner:** {{owner}}

## 1. Purpose

This policy makes sure vendors who access {{company}} systems or data meet our security and data protection requirements.

## 2. Vendor inventory and tiers

All vendors are recorded in the vendor inventory with an owner and a risk tier:

| Tier | Criteria | Review frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Processes customer personal data or is essential to service delivery | Annually |
| High | Accesses internal confidential data or production systems | Annually |
| Medium | Limited access to internal data | Every 2 years |
| Low | No access to data or systems | At onboarding only |

## 3. Onboarding

Before a Critical or High vendor is engaged:

1. The vendor completes a security questionnaire or provides an independent report (ISO 27001 certificate, SOC 2 report).
2. The security lead reviews the results and records the decision.
3. A contract is signed with security requirements, breach notification duties, audit rights and, where personal data is shared, a Data Processing Agreement covering DPDP obligations.

## 4. Ongoing monitoring

Vendors are reassessed according to their tier. Significant changes, incidents or expiring certifications trigger a new review.

## 5. Offboarding

When a relationship ends, access is revoked and the vendor confirms return or erasure of {{company}} data.

## 6. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually.
