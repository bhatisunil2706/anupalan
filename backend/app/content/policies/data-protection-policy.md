# Data Protection and Privacy Policy

**Company:** {{company}}
**Owner:** {{owner}}

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how {{company}} processes digital personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the DPDP Rules, 2025, and other applicable privacy laws.

## 2. Principles

1. **Lawful ground:** we process personal data only with valid consent or for a legitimate use allowed by Section 7 of the DPDP Act.
2. **Notice:** we give a clear, itemised notice before or when asking for consent.
3. **Purpose limitation and minimisation:** we collect only the data needed for the stated purpose.
4. **Accuracy:** we keep personal data accurate and complete where it is used for decisions or shared.
5. **Storage limitation:** we erase personal data when the purpose ends or consent is withdrawn, unless the law requires us to keep it.
6. **Security:** we protect personal data with reasonable security safeguards.
7. **Accountability:** we remain responsible for processing done by our vendors on our behalf.

## 3. Data inventory

The privacy contact maintains an inventory of all processing activities, including data categories, purposes, lawful ground, systems, processors, retention periods and transfers outside India. It is reviewed every 6 months and when new processing starts.

## 4. Consent

Consent is free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, given through a clear affirmative action. Users can withdraw consent as easily as they gave it. We keep records of notice versions and consent.

## 5. Rights of Data Principals

We support the rights to access information, correction and erasure, grievance redressal and nomination. Requests are logged, identity is verified, and responses are sent within our internal target of 30 days and never later than 90 days for grievances. Every response includes our privacy contact details.

## 6. Children's data

{{company}} does not knowingly process children's personal data unless verifiable parental consent has been obtained. We do not track, behaviourally monitor or target advertising at children.

## 7. Processors and transfers

We share personal data with processors only under a contract that includes security and data protection obligations. Transfers outside India are recorded and checked against any restrictions notified by the Government and sector regulators.

## 8. Privacy contact

{{company}}'s privacy contact or Data Protection Officer is published on our website and app.

## 9. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually.
