# Data Retention and Disposal Policy

**Company:** {{company}}
**Owner:** {{owner}}

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how long {{company}} keeps information and how it is securely disposed of.

## 2. Retention schedule

| Information | Retention | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer personal data | While the account is active, then erased within 90 days unless legally required | DPDP Act s.8(7) |
| Security and access logs | At least 1 year, stored in India | DPDP Rules R.6(e); CERT-In Directions (v) requires 180 days |
| Incident records | 5 years | Internal requirement |
| Employee records | As required by labour and tax laws | Applicable law |
| Financial records | 8 years | Companies Act / Income Tax Act |
| Backups | 35 days rolling | Internal requirement |

Owners of each information type may set shorter periods where the purpose ends sooner.

## 3. Erasure

1. Personal data is erased when the purpose is complete, consent is withdrawn, or the retention period ends, unless the law requires us to keep it.
2. Where the DPDP Rules require, users are informed at least 48 hours before erasure.
3. Processors are instructed to erase data in the same way, and confirm erasure.

## 4. Secure disposal

1. Digital media is securely wiped or cryptographically erased before re-use or disposal.
2. Devices that cannot be wiped are physically destroyed by an approved vendor, who provides a certificate.
3. Paper records containing confidential or personal data are shredded.

## 5. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually.
