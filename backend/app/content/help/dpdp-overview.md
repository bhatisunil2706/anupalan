The **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** and the **DPDP Rules, 2025** govern how organisations handle digital personal data in India. The platform covers the Act as a framework (49 requirements) and gives you working tools for the duties that need day-to-day operations.

> This page explains the platform, not the law. For legal advice on your situation, speak to a privacy lawyer.

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Data Principal | The person the data is about: your customer, user or employee |
| Data Fiduciary | You: the organisation deciding why and how data is processed |
| Data Processor | A vendor processing data on your behalf (cloud host, email provider, payroll) |
| Significant Data Fiduciary (SDF) | A fiduciary notified by the government for extra duties (DPO in India, audits, DPIAs) |
| Consent Manager | A registered intermediary through which people can give, manage and withdraw consent |
| Data Protection Board (DPB) | The regulator that receives breach reports and handles complaints |

## Your main duties and where to handle them

| Duty | Platform module |
| --- | --- |
| Know what personal data you process, why, on what basis and with whom | [Data inventory](/docs/data-inventory) |
| Find personal data you didn't know about | [Data discovery](/docs/data-discovery) |
| Give a clear notice and get valid consent; make withdrawal as easy | [Consent records](/docs/consent-records) |
| Respond to access, correction, erasure, nomination, withdrawal and grievance requests | [Rights requests](/docs/rights-requests) |
| Report personal data breaches to the Board and tell affected people | [Incidents](/docs/incidents) |
| Have reasonable security safeguards | The security controls across [Controls](/docs/controls) |
| Bind processors by contract and check them | [Vendors](/docs/vendors) and the DPDP processor questionnaire |
| Keep data only as long as needed | PRV-08 Retention and erasure, and the retention field in the inventory |
| Train staff | The "India's DPDP Act for everyone at work" course in [Training](/docs/training) |

## Phased start dates

The DPDP Rules come into force in phases: the Data Protection Board from 13 November 2025, Consent Manager provisions from 13 November 2026, and the remaining obligations from 13 May 2027. The DPDP framework page in the platform shows the requirements with this context.

## Deadlines the platform tracks

| Deadline | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Personal data breach: report to the Board without delay, with a detailed report within 72 hours | Incident clocks and the dashboard |
| Rights requests: within your own service level, never more than 90 days | Rights request due dates |
| CERT-In (a separate law, often triggered by the same incident): report within 6 hours | Incident clocks |

## DPDP-specific profile questions

Answer these in **Settings → Organisation → What applies to you** to switch on the matching requirements: processing children's data, being a Significant Data Fiduciary, and using a Consent Manager. See [Frameworks](/docs/frameworks#conditional-requirements-and-profile-questions).
