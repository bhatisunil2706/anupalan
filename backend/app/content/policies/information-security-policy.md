# Information Security Policy

**Company:** {{company}}
**Owner:** {{owner}}
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors and third parties with access to {{company}} information or systems.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how {{company}} protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the information it holds, including personal data of customers and employees. It is the top-level policy of our information security programme. Topic-specific policies support it.

## 2. Objectives

{{company}} commits to:

1. Protect customer and company information from unauthorised access, change or loss.
2. Meet legal, regulatory and contractual requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules, the CERT-In Directions of April 2022, and customer contracts.
3. Identify and treat information security risks on a regular basis.
4. Report and respond to security incidents quickly, including reporting to CERT-In within 6 hours and to the Data Protection Board as required.
5. Continually improve the information security programme.

Leadership sets measurable security objectives each year and reviews progress at least annually.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

- **Leadership** approves this policy, provides resources and reviews the programme at least once a year.
- **Security lead** runs the programme, maintains policies, coordinates risk assessments and audits, and reports to leadership.
- **Privacy contact / Data Protection Officer** owns DPDP obligations, data principal requests and breach notifications.
- **Control owners** operate the controls assigned to them and keep evidence current.
- **All staff** follow security policies, complete training, and report suspected incidents immediately.

## 4. Policy framework

This policy is supported by topic-specific policies, including Acceptable Use, Access Control, Incident Response, Data Protection and Privacy, Data Retention, Vendor Management, and Business Continuity. All policies are reviewed at least annually and after significant change.

## 5. Compliance and exceptions

Compliance with security policies is checked through internal audits and automated monitoring. Exceptions must be requested in writing, risk-assessed, approved by the security lead and recorded with an expiry date. Violations may lead to disciplinary action.

## 6. Review

This policy is reviewed at least every 12 months by the security lead and approved by leadership.
