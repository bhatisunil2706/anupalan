Policies are the written rules auditors read first. The platform manages their whole life: drafting, approval, staff acknowledgment, yearly review and evidence.

## Adding a policy

**Policies → New policy** gives four ways in:

| Option | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Upload a document** | You already have policies in Word (.docx), PDF, Markdown, text or HTML. Headings, lists, bold and tables are converted so you can edit them here. PDFs keep text only, so check the formatting. Scanned PDFs have no text; paste instead. Old .doc files must be saved as .docx first. |
| **Paste from Google Docs or Word** | Copy the whole document (Ctrl/Cmd+A, then Ctrl/Cmd+C) and paste into the editor. Formatting is kept; scripts and styles are stripped. |
| **Write from scratch** | Use the editor's toolbar: headings, bold, italic, links, lists, quotes, dividers and tables. No formatting codes needed. |
| **Start from a template** | Eight templates written for Indian companies, covering DPDP and CERT-In: information security, access control, acceptable use, data protection, data retention, incident response, business continuity and vendor management. |

When saving, choose the **owner** (who keeps it up to date) and the **controls it supports**. Imported files are converted (Word and PDF on the server, other formats in your browser) and the file itself is never stored; only the text you save is kept.

The editor has a **Markdown** view for people who prefer it, and an **Import file** button to replace the content from a document at any time.

## Approval and versions

- A new policy starts as a **Draft**.
- An admin clicks **Approve version 1**. The policy becomes **Approved**, gets a review date (usually one year ahead), and is attached as evidence to every control it supports.
- Editing an approved policy creates a new **draft version** (version 2 and so on). The approved version stays in force as evidence until the new one is approved.

## Staff acknowledgment

When a policy is approved, everyone in the organisation is asked to read and acknowledge it (it appears on their dashboard under **Policies to read and acknowledge**). Each new version needs a fresh acknowledgment. The policies list shows how many people have acknowledged the current version, for example "18 of 22".

## Yearly review

When a policy's review date approaches, an **Annual review** task is created for its owner. Review it, make any changes and approve again.

## Other actions

- **Download (.md)** saves the policy text.
- **Delete** (admins only) removes the policy and its acknowledgments.
- The [AI assistant](/docs/ai-assistant), if your owner has turned it on, can draft a policy from your existing policies and controls.
