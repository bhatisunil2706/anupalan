# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pramaanly to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their experience, background, identity or role.
We pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience for all.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assume good intent, and help newcomers.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and other channels) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Sunil Bhati, through a private message on GitHub
([@bhatisunil2706](https://github.com/bhatisunil2706)). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

This code is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
